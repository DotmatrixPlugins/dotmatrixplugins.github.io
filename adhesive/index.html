The classic bus glue, without the digital grit.

Adhesive rebuilds the SL 4000 G bus compressor from the console's own schematics: the two-VCA feedback design, 2:1, 4:1 and 10:1, attack from 0.1 to 30 ms and the famous Auto release, with a backlit needle that shows every dB. Put it on your mix bus at 4:1, 30 ms, Auto, and lower the threshold until the needle dances around 1 to 3 dB. In front of it sits a complete G-series channel strip, and all of it is built to stay alias-free.

What it does

  • The G-series bus compressor: 2:1 / 4:1 / 10:1, 0.1 to 30 ms attack, 0.1 to 1.2 s or Auto release
  • Make-up, parallel mix and a side-chain high-pass that keeps sub-bass from pumping the mix
  • A full channel strip: 291 mic amp saturation, 292 pink or 242 black EQ, filters, compressor and expander/gate, VCA fader
  • Clean by design: matched EQ curves, 4x oversampling by default (up to 8x), anti-aliased saturation
  • 22 factory presets: classic glue, master bus, drums, lead synth and bass
  • Wide or vertical (SSL console strip) layout, dark or light panel, resizable from 60 to 200 %

AU, VST3 and CLAP for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.14 or later).
