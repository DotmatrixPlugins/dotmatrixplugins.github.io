Basement

Two sources, one pad, endless motion. A whole synth on one panel.

Get Basement at SoundUplinkUser guide (PDF)

Draw the wave. Draw the motion.

Basement is a morphing synthesizer built like a single hardware unit. Switch on Draw Osc and drag across the pad to draw your own waveform, then switch it off and record a path on the same XY pad to move the sound in time with the bar.

What it does

  • Draw Osc: draw one cycle of your own wave and play it straight away
  • XY pad that records your movement as a loop, per note or across the whole part
  • Two sources from 36 waves, your drawn wave or noise
  • Ladder and multimode filters, with 4x oversampling for clean drive and FM
  • 16-step arpeggiator and sequencer with ties, slides and chords

AU, VST3, CLAP and a stand-alone app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
