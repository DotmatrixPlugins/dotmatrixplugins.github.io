What we make

Classic sound, rebuilt from the circuit up.

Dotmatrix makes audio plug-ins for producers, mixing engineers and mastering engineers.

Each one starts from the sound of classic studio hardware: a 1975 mixing console, a 2-inch multitrack tape machine, a classic bus compressor, a hardware sampler. We study how the gear behaves and rebuild it from the circuit up, then add what the original never had: mid/side processing, morphing, recall, and controls designed for working fast.

Every release shares one design language, ships with a full user guide, and runs as AU, VST3 and CLAP on macOS.

How we work

Remote, focused, unhurried.

Dotmatrix is a small, independent studio that works remotely. We care about the work, not the hours or the location.

Days are planned around focus, with long uninterrupted stretches for design, DSP and listening. Meetings are short and only happen when they're needed.

Every release goes through careful listening tests and a full quality check before it ships. We'd rather get it right than get it out fast.

Vaccination policy: there is no vaccination requirement. Everyone who works with Dotmatrix, employees and contractors alike, works remotely, so there is no shared workplace.
