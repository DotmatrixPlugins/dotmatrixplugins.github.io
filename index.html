Workflow

  1. 01

    Schematic

    Service manuals, circuit diagrams and the maker's own measurements of the machine. Where none exist, physics and measurement.

  2. 02

    Model

    Each stage solved as a circuit and checked against the paper: Spulentonband's reproduce card matches its schematic within 0.005 dB.

  3. 03

    Clean

    Oversampled and anti-aliased at the source, so the character is the circuit's, not the converter's. Measured, not assumed.

  4. 04

    Release

    AU, VST3 and CLAP, universal for Apple Silicon and Intel, with factory presets, a printed-style guide and a film.
