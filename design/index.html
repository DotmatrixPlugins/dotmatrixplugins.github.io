01 · Featured project

Dotmatrix. One brand, end to end.

Dotmatrix makes audio plug-ins for macOS. I designed and art-directed the whole of it: the identity, eleven instruments that look and feel like real hardware, a film for every release, the guides and the website.

Each plug-in starts as a real piece of studio gear: a Studer tape machine, a Harrison console strip, an SSL bus compressor. The job was to keep the feel of the hardware and make it easier to read. Every interface comes from one design system of panels, dot-pointer knobs, glass displays and glow-ring feature dials, so all eleven belong together and each has its own colour.

  • Identity · the gradient D mark, the dot-matrix wordmark and the full lockup set
  • Interface design · eleven plug-ins in a shared system, each with a light and a dark panel
  • Motion · a 30-second promo film for every release, with cinematic close-ups
  • Web · dotmatrixaudio.com in English and Japanese, rebuilt with every release
  • Print · a designed user guide for each plug-in
