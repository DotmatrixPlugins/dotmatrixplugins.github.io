Analoger32

The Harrison 32-series strip, modelled from its own circuit drawing.

Get Analoger32 at SoundUplinkUser guide (PDF)

Broad, musical bells, the famous filter bump and a Jensen front end.

Analoger32 is a channel strip and mix strip modelled on the Harrison 32-series, the 1975 console whose EQ became one of the most loved in recording history. Its four EQ bands and its high- and low-pass filters come from Harrison's own 1977 drawing of the console's input module, component for component, so the proportional Q and the filter lift are the circuit's own. Put it on a kick, a bass, a vocal or a whole group, shape it with four broad, overlapping bands, and roll the low end off with the high-pass: just above the corner it lifts by about a decibel and a quarter, keeping the weight while the mud goes.

What it does

  • Four bands (LO, LO-MID, HI-MID, HI) with the 32-series proportional Q: broad on small moves, tighter as you push
  • LO and HI switch between gentle 6 dB/oct shelves and bells
  • Harrison high- and low-pass filters, 12 dB/oct, with the signature lift at the corner
  • Jensen-transformer mic preamp (20 to 70 dB, -20 pad), HiZ instrument input and line input with trim
  • Fader with 0 dB switch, pan and the stereo mix bus, as on the 32Classic MS Mix Strip
  • A live response curve drawn from the circuit, bi-colour LEDs and a dBu peak read-out
  • The strip's own noise at Harrison's spec; 4x oversampling by default (up to 8x)
  • 21 factory presets: console, drums, bass, vocals, instruments and character
  • Dark or light panel, resizable from 60 to 200 %

AU, VST3 and CLAP for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.14 or later).
