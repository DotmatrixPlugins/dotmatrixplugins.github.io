Shirabe

Click a pack. Get a melody.

Click a pack, get a melody, and teach it your taste.

Shirabe is a melody writer. Shirabe is Japanese for a tune. Click a seed pack and it writes a new melody in that style, in your key and at your tempo. Every click is a new one, and it all runs on your Mac: no downloads, no account.

Already have an idea? Play it in, drop a MIDI file or an audio loop, and RE-IMAGINE rewrites it as far as CREATIVITY lets it stray. Shape the result, rate it, and Shirabe learns what you like. It plays in time through its own synth, or sends only MIDI to any instrument.

What it does

  • 16 seed packs, from Koto Garden and Cinematic to Lo-Fi Study, Trap Bells, Afro Bounce and Trance Lift, plus an 8-pack bonus bank
  • Any folder of your own MIDI files becomes a pack
  • Melodies built in phrases that repeat and answer each other, over chord progressions, in any key and 16 scales, Japanese scales among them
  • RE-IMAGINE from a dropped MIDI file, an audio loop of up to 16 bars, a recording from your keys, or its own output
  • An editable piano roll, and 64 takes of history with undo and redo
  • SHAPE without rewriting: gate, rhythm, contour, arpeggios on four grids in four orders, swing, humanise and octave
  • LIKE and PASS train taste models of your own, which you can switch between
  • A built-in synth with 16 sounds (koto, harp, shamisen, tine keys, glass bells, pads, leads and basses) and reverb, or MIDI only
  • HOST SYNC locked to your project's bars; trigger and transpose from your keys, with LATCH
  • DRAG MIDI and DRAG WAV onto a track, named with key, scale and tempo
  • Dark or light panel, resizable from 60 to 200 %

AU instrument with MIDI out, VST3 and CLAP, and a standalone app, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.14 or later).
