Dubwise

A tape echo and a dub desk in one unit, built for the send.

Get Dubwise at SoundUplinkUser guide (PDF)

Linked filters. Reverse dubs.

Dubwise brings the feel of a tape echo and a dub mixing desk into one unit. Link the Low Cut and High Cut and sweep the band through the echoes for controlled dubs, or flip on Reverse and let each repeat play back the other way.

What it does

  • Linked Low Cut and High Cut: sweep the band pass as one
  • Reverse dub, with its own mix against the echoes
  • Tempo-locked echoes: note divisions, dotted or straight
  • Wow and flutter for the wobble of a worn tape machine
  • Flanger and a big reverb with freeze, plus 30 factory presets

AU, VST3 and CLAP for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.14 or later).
