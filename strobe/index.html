Strobe

Turn any held sound into a pulsing part that starts on the bar.

Get Strobe at SoundUplinkUser guide (PDF)

Chop any sound to the bar.

Strobe chops any sound into a pattern locked to your session's tempo. Paint steps around the step wheel and a held pad, chord or synth note turns into a pulsing, stuttering part that starts exactly on the bar.

What it does

  • Step wheel with 4 to 64 steps per bar
  • Starts from the downbeat whenever the input crosses the threshold
  • Attack, decay, sustain and release on every step, from hard chops to soft pumps
  • 100 factory patterns across ten styles, or randomise a new one
  • Resonant filter, three-band EQ and a 30 Hz cut

AU, VST3, CLAP and a stand-alone app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.14 or later).
